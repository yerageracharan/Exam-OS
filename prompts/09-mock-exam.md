# Mock Examination

**Use:** After substantial syllabus coverage and practice.

~~~text
Create and administer a mock exam for [MODULE] using the verified exam format and course materials.

Exam facts:
- Duration: [DURATION]
- Total marks: [MARKS]
- Question types: [FORMAT]
- Scope: [LECTURES / CHAPTERS]
- Allowed resources: [DETAILS / UNKNOWN]

Before creating the paper, check the uploaded materials for the format and scope. If a detail is not provided, label your assumption.

DURING THE MOCK
- Present the entire paper in one organised batch or sections that match the real exam.
- Keep questions hidden from the answer key until submission.
- Use official questions only when explicitly requested and preserve their wording; otherwise label all new questions as generated.
- Do not give hints or answers mid-attempt unless I ask to stop the mock.

AFTER SUBMISSION
- Mark each question and explain the reasoning.
- Show total marks, percentage, and breakdown by topic.
- Identify concept gaps, careless errors and time-management issues separately.
- Compare performance against the actual mark scheme if one was provided. Do not invent an official marking rubric.
- Give a remediation plan and a short re-test for the weakest areas.
~~~
