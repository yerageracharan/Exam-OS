# Resume a Study Session

**Use:** When returning after a break or moving to a new chat.

~~~text
Resume my [MODULE] exam preparation from the current files and the study record available in this conversation.

First, verify the last completed lecture/topic, last official question ID attempted, answers submitted, marking outcome, unresolved errors, and next planned step. Do not assume a task is complete merely because it was discussed.

Give me a short checkpoint:
- Last confirmed topic completed:
- Last confirmed question attempted:
- Last verified score:
- Weak concepts still open:
- Remaining source material:
- Next best action:

If any detail cannot be verified from the conversation or source, label it “unknown” rather than reconstructing it from memory.

Then continue from the next logical step without restarting the course intake or asking for information already available.
~~~
