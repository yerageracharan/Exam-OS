# Error Diagnosis and Remediation

**Use:** After repeated mistakes or a weak mock-exam section.

~~~text
Analyse my mistakes from [QUESTION IDS / PRACTICE BATCH / MOCK EXAM].

For each error:
- Identify the exact question and my submitted answer.
- State the correct answer and source/derivation.
- Diagnose the error type: concept, notation, formula selection, calculation, misreading, logic, memory, time pressure, or unsupported guess.
- Identify the underlying misunderstanding, not just the immediate wrong step.
- Re-teach the concept using a different analogy or visual method.
- Show one corrected worked example.
- Give me 2–4 targeted questions that test the same skill in new contexts.

Then group errors into recurring patterns and rank them by impact and frequency. Do not label a weakness as fixed until I answer a re-test correctly with a sound explanation. Keep the old error in the log and mark it resolved only after evidence.
~~~
