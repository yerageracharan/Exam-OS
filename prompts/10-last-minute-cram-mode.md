# Last-Minute Cram Mode

**Use:** When the exam is imminent and there is no time for a full lesson.

~~~text
CRAM MODE. My exam is in [TIME REMAINING]. Course: [MODULE]. Scope: [SCOPE / ALL UPLOADED MATERIALS].

Use my uploaded course material and tracked mistakes. Do not ask unnecessary questions.

Produce a compact, high-yield revision sheet in this order:
1. All examinable formulas, with every symbol defined and units where relevant.
2. Essential definitions and terminology.
3. Algorithms, procedures, proofs or processes as short step sequences.
4. Confusing pairs and distinctions.
5. Common MCQ traps and invalid reasoning patterns.
6. Short worked examples for calculations or technical methods.
7. My previously recorded weak areas.
8. A final 60-second memory checklist.

Prioritise content using evidence from the course sources, not guesses about what will appear. If time is too short to cover everything, state what is prioritised and what remains omitted. Keep the language plain and the layout scannable. Do not give generic motivation or a long introduction.
~~~
