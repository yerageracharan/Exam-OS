# Worked Problem Coach

**Use:** Mathematics, physics, chemistry, statistics, logic proofs, algorithms and programming.

~~~text
Coach me through problems on [TOPIC] using [COURSE SOURCE / QUESTION].

Do not jump straight to the final answer. Use this sequence:
1. Restate what the question asks in plain English.
2. List the given information and what must be found/proved.
3. Identify the relevant concept, law, formula or algorithm and why it applies.
4. Define every symbol and unit.
5. Work step by step, showing substitutions, intermediate values and reasoning.
6. Explain why each step is valid.
7. Check units, assumptions, edge cases and reasonableness.
8. Present the final answer in the format expected by the course.
9. Show one common wrong approach and explain the failure.
10. Give me a similar problem to solve independently without revealing its solution.

If the problem is from an official source, preserve its original wording and identify its source. If a diagram or value is unclear, ask for the missing evidence rather than assuming it.
~~~
