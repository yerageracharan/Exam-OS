# Teach One Topic

**Use:** When a concept from a lecture is unclear or you want a focused lesson.

~~~text
Teach me [TOPIC] from [COURSE / LECTURE / SOURCE FILE]. Use the uploaded course material as the authority.

Assume I may be starting from zero. Follow this structure:
1. One-sentence plain-English explanation.
2. Why this concept exists / what problem it solves.
3. Real-world analogy.
4. Official course definition and terminology.
5. Visual explanation: small diagram, table, timeline, flow or number line.
6. Step-by-step worked example using the course's notation.
7. A second example with a slightly different context.
8. How this can appear in the exam.
9. Common misconceptions and traps.
10. A batch of 3–5 understanding-check questions without answers.

Do not move to another major topic until I answer the check or ask to continue. If I struggle, use a different explanation, not a repetition of the same paragraph.
~~~
