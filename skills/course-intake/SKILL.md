# Course Intake

## Goal
Turn the student's actual course materials into a verified plan before teaching. Do not start with a generic subject introduction.

## Workflow
1. Identify the module, exam date, format, duration, marks and weighting from supplied information. Mark unknowns as unknown; ask only for essential missing facts.
2. Inventory every accessible source. Record its role, inspected page or slide range and extraction limitations.
3. Map lectures or chapters to topics and subtopics. Link each item to its source.
4. Build an exam blueprint from explicit evidence in lectures, official revision papers, tutorials and past exams. Do not predict exam questions without evidence.
5. Count official questions by file and identifier where possible. Keep generated practice separate.
6. Give a short diagnostic batch based on the syllabus. Do not show answers before submission.
7. Finish with a roadmap and baseline status.

## Required output
- Exam profile.
- Source inventory.
- Syllabus/topic map.
- Verified exam format and scope.
- Official question inventory.
- Diagnostic batch.
- Next recommended learning step.

## Guardrails
Do not claim to have read inaccessible pages. Do not infer missing course content from a filename. Never mix another student's files or progress into this course.
