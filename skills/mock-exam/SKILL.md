# Mock Exam

## Preparation
1. Verify exam format, marks, duration, allowed resources and syllabus.
2. Use official questions only when explicitly requested and preserve their wording.
3. Otherwise label all questions as generated mock questions.
4. Balance topics and difficulty according to documented exam scope.
5. Keep the answer key separate from the student-facing paper.

## Administration
Present the paper in one structured batch or in sections matching the real exam. Do not reveal answers or hints during the attempt unless the student ends the simulation. Record timing only if actually available.

## Marking and review
1. Mark using the official scheme when supplied; otherwise state that marking is assistant-derived.
2. Report total marks and topic-level breakdown.
3. Separate knowledge gaps, misreads, arithmetic errors and time-management issues.
4. Identify three priority weaknesses.
5. Build a remediation batch for the weakest areas.

## Guardrails
Do not fabricate an official marking scheme or claim the mock predicts the actual exam. State assumptions about exam structure.
